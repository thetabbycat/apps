# HeadUp App Support



Welcome, AvGeek! Thanks for using HeadUp. This page is designed to help you get the most out of the app and answer any common questions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

#### **1. The app and widget say "No Aircraft Nearby." Is it broken?**

No, this is expected behavior! HeadUp shows you **live** air traffic. If there are no aircraft transmitting their position within the scan radius you've set, the app will correctly report that the skies are clear.

**What you can do:**

*   **Increase Scan Radius:** Go into the HeadUp app's settings (the gear icon) and increase the "Scan Radius" slider. A larger radius is more likely to pick up aircraft, especially if you are not near an airport or a major flight path.
*   **Be Patient:** Air traffic is dynamic. Try checking again in a few minutes.
*   **Use Manual Location:** If you live in a remote area, you can turn off "Use Automatic Location" in the settings and manually set a location over a busier area (like a major airport) to see the app in action.

---

#### **2. The widget isn't updating in real-time. Why?**

This is a limitation of Apple's WidgetKit framework, not the app itself. To preserve battery life, iOS widgets are only allowed to refresh periodically (typically every 5-15 minutes).

*   The widget is designed for **glanceable, periodic updates**, not for minute-by-minute live tracking.
*   You can always tap the **refresh button** on the widget to request an immediate update from the system.

---

#### **3. How do I change the information shown on the widget?**

You have full control!

1.  Open the **HeadUp app**.
2.  Tap the **gear icon** to go to Settings.
3.  Under the "Widget Content" section, you can toggle on or off the Altitude, Speed, Heading, and Route.
4.  The changes will appear on your Medium and Large widgets the next time they refresh.

---

## Troubleshooting

*   **Location Not Working:** If the app can't get your location, you may have accidentally denied permission. You can fix this by going to your iPhone's **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > HeadUp** and ensuring it is set to "While Using the App".
*   **Widget Is Stuck or Frozen:** Sometimes widgets can get stuck. The classic fix usually works: simply remove the widget from your Home Screen and add it again.
